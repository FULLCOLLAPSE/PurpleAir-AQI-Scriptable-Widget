Segregated: The light sets each channel in turn and triggers the camera shutter once per channel via the 3.5mm sync port. You get 3 (or 4) separate images to combine in post. Tether to Capture One — each frame auto-imports.
Combined: Set your camera to Bulb mode. The app opens the shutter, flashes R → G → B in sequence during the exposure, then closes it — one image with all channels. Good for situations where post-combining isn't practical.
